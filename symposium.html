The day

What a symposium looks like

The monthly meetup is for keeping in touch. The symposium is where work gets presented properly: longer talks, an external keynote, posters and demos with real time to look at them, and enough of a programme that it is worth inviting colleagues and partners from outside the community.

It is also the community's internal publication venue. Groups present what they have built, students get a real audience and a line for their CV, and the whole university gets a picture of what is happening here.

Programme

Six tracks

Tracks exist to help you find the sessions worth your morning — not to police what counts as relevant. If your work spans two, say so and we will place it.

Agents in research

Applications across science and engineering: what was attempted, what worked, and what the results actually support.

Engineering & systems

Architectures, tooling, infrastructure, cost and observability — the practical craft of making agents run reliably.

Evaluation, safety & policy

Measurement, failure analysis, red-teaming, and the governance questions that come with autonomy.

Teaching & the student experience

Agents in the classroom and the lab, assessment, and student-led work.

Industry & deployment

Partner organisations and university units on what it takes to move from a pilot to something operational.

Work in progress

Deliberately unfinished work, brought for feedback rather than applause. Often the most useful track in the room.
